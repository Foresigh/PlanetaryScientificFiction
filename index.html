The Premise

A New Genre of Planetary Fiction

Planetary science fiction expands the scale of storytelling beyond planets as backdrops — making worlds themselves the protagonists, the vessels, the stakes.

"What happens when humanity does not leave Earth — but moves it?"

The Earth Mover Universe poses a fundamental question that inverts the classic science fiction premise. Rather than abandoning our origin to seek new worlds, humanity engineers the ultimate act of survival — transforming the planet itself into a vessel for deep space travel.

The consequences ripple across politics, faith, science, and the very identity of what it means to be human — carrying an entire civilization, its history, its beauty, and its conflict into the stars.
