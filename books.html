The Earth Mover Universe

The Complete Series

Four novels and a hardcover omnibus — the full scope of humanity's most ambitious journey told world by world.

Reading Order

The Earth Mover Novels

Begin at Book One or enter the universe at any point. Each novel expands the consequences of humanity's unprecedented decision.

Book One
Earth Mover
"One man. One world. No way back."

The origin of everything. A single man, a planetary crisis, and the most consequential decision in human history. Earth Mover introduces the concept, the technology, the politics, and the human cost of setting an entire world in motion. The question at the heart of the series begins here: if you could save humanity by moving its home — should you?

Purchase → Free Sample
Book Two
Earth Mover Two
"The consequences of moving a planet unfold."

Earth is in motion and the cracks are showing. Visionaries and opportunists clash as civilization buckles under the strain of perpetual displacement. The second volume explores what happens not in the moment of decision, but in the years of living with it — politically, spiritually, technologically. The cost of survival is always more than you planned.

Purchase →
Book Three
Earth Mover Universe
"The scope expands to planetary consequence."

The universe reveals itself. Civilizations measured not by cities or nations, but world by world. The Earth Mover Universe novel takes the series to its widest scope — a universe where planets are the fundamental unit of power, identity, and conflict. Humanity is not alone in having moved a world, and not every civilization has good intentions.

Purchase →
Book Four
The Good Earth
"New stories from a traveling Earth society."

The final novel shifts focus inward — not to the cosmic stakes, but to the human texture of life inside a civilization in permanent motion. New characters, new voices, new questions. What does art mean when your world is moving? What does faith look like when you carry your heaven with you? The Good Earth is an intimate conclusion to an epic series.
