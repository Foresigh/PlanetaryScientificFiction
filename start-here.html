The Concept

What Is Planetary Science Fiction?

A genre where the planet itself becomes the protagonist, the vessel, and the stage. This is not about leaving Earth — it is about what happens when you refuse to leave without it.

🏠
World as Spacecraft

Earth is not the destination. Earth is the ship. Everything — every person, every history, every tree — travels into deep space together.

⚛
Civilizational Stakes

The conflict isn't between characters — it's between the forces that define what humanity will become when the ground itself is always moving.

🔍
Grounded Speculation

Built on real science, real physics, real human psychology — stretched to a scale that demands new thinking about what we are and where we're going.

Common Questions

Reader FAQ

Do I need to read in order?

The series rewards sequential reading — each book builds on the last. But each novel is designed to be an entry point. Start wherever the concept grabs you.

Is this hard science fiction?

It is grounded in real physics and science, but the priority is narrative and human consequence — not technical specification. Think Kim Stanley Robinson more than Arthur C. Clarke.

What formats are available?

Digital editions are available through Payhip. The hardcover omnibus collects all four novels in a single physical volume.

Is the series complete?

Yes. All four novels are written and available. The Earth Mover Universe tells a complete story with a beginning, middle, and end.
